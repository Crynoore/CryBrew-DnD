[[Buns Ruben]] forrest gnome originally from the Feywild born in the Whistle hollow druid grove, has a brother known as [[Himothy Ruben]]. Seperated at +-75 years old, [[Buns Ruben]] found himself captured in the Feydark by a Darkling empire. After many years of enslavement Buns escaped via a fey crossing to the material plane. A whole new world full of new rules completely alien to him he found himself a friend named Thovig, Thovig was a powerful human former slave owner and master tactician who used an iconic round table to construct plans of how to overthrow the next unlucky village ready to join his empire of slaves. He had a change of heart however once his kid managed to sneak his way in to the famous culling pit so deep it reached the Underdark itself and got mauled by Carrion Crawlers. From that fateful day onward Thovig vowed to abolish all slave his and other’s camps and constructed a guild, known as Thovigs Round Table in reference to were his biggest mistakes in life where decided, the dreaded round table. As Ruben’s and Thovig’s views on slavery aligned they began to construct a mighty army of mostly human, dwarfish and gnomish knights with more joining with each camp they abolished. Thovig died at 80 years old due to old age, this was right before the “Great Evil”. After the great evil the new leadership in the Round Table decided to join a greater effort known as [[The Imperial Brotherhood]], joining the the [[Shattered Abodes of Many]] and [[Singni-us]] to prevent another “Great Evil” from happening.
