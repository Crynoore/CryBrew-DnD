# Character Creation

every species has a set amount of points to buy perks with and start with a race specific perk 

  

there are 2 sets of perks, one that give you buffs but require points and one that give you debuffs/ flaws/ nerfs that give you points 

  

{simple exemple: a perk that gives you +2 hp per level would cost 5 perk points, and a perk that would make you move 1m slower would give you 4 perk points}

## Perks 

(pp is Perk point)

list of perks

you cant take opposites of a perk/ you cant take both the positive and the negative prek

### Positive Perks 

- gain a General Action (cost 12pp)
- gain 1m of movement speed (cost 8pp)
- gain a proficiency in a skill (cost 3pp)
- Gain an extra time slot while resting (cost 7pp)
- gain a +1 to a stat (costs 6pp)

### Negative Perks

- 1 to a stat (gain 7pp)
- 1 ac (gain 5pp)
- 2 hp per level (gain 7pp)
- lose a time slot while resting (gain 5pp)
- 1m of movement speed (gain 5pp)
- 1 bonus action [cant go in the negative] (gain 6pp)
- gain a vulnerability (gain 10pp)
- Illiterate: cant read/learn any language (gain 2pp)
- Unlucky: you always have disadvantage (gain 9pp)
