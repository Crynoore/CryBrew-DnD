| Cosmic Nebula                    |
| -------------------------------- |
| **Casting Time:** 1 action       |
| **Range:** 120 feet              |
| **Target:** A point within range |
| **Components:** V, S             |
| **Spell level:** 1               |


summons a nebula the contains a lot of different materials.
deals 1d6 dmg the results form your die correlates to what happens {look at the list below}

- 1= summons a small Vacuum: on hit suck all creatures within a 30ft radius to it, every creature has to do a dex save, on a fail they get sucked to the point, if they succeed they stay still
- 2= deals 1d8 cold damage
- 3=roll 2d6 as Force damage
- 4= summons collection(4) of comets that homes into the closes being that is not its self. dealing 1d4 per comets
- 5= summons a beautiful image made out of space dust
- 6= summons a sun that deals 2d4 fire damage
