| Rock Throw   |                 |
| ------------ | --------------- |
| Spell level  | 1 transmutation |
| Casting Time | 1 action        |
| Range        | 120 ft          |
| Components   | S, M (rock)     |
| Duration     | Instantaneous   |
| Classes      | Druid, Sorcerer |
A stone tears itself free from the nearby soil and hurls toward a creature of your choice within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage and must succeed on a strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, for every spell slot level above 1st you may target one additional creature with this spell.**
