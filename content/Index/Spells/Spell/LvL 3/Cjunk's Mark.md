| Cjunk's Mark                |
| --------------------------- |
| **Casting Time:** 1 action  |
| **Range:** 90 feet          |
| **Duration:** Instantaneous |
| **School:** Divination      |
| **Spell level:** 3          |
You magically mark one creature you can see within range as your quarry. Until the spell ends, your allies and you deal an extra 1d6 Force damage to the target whenever you hit it with an attack roll.
