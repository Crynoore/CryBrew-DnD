| Intercontinental Magic Missal |                        |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Spell level                   | 6 evocation            |
| Casting Time                  | 1 action               |
| Range                         | Unlimited (same plane) |
| Components                    | S, V                   |
| Duration                      | Instantaneous          |
| Classes                       |                        |
Choose a creature, object, or location you are familiar with, or one whose name and general location you know. The missile travels unerringly, bypassing all non-magical obstacles and ignoring distance so long as the target is on the same plane.

After a brief delay 1d4 rounds, the missile strikes.

Creatures in the area must make a Dex saving throw, On a failure take 5d10 in force and 5d10 fire damage and Half damage On a success in addition Structures and objects automatically take **maximum damage**.
