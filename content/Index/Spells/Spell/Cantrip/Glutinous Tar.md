| Glutinous Tar                    |
| -------------------------------- |
| **Casting Time:** 1 bonus action |
| **Range:** 60 feet               |
| **Components:** V, S             |
| **Spell level:**0                |

summons a pool of sentient tar near a Creature, this tar deal 1d4 necrotic damage to the Creature at the end of your turn, if the tar is set ablaze it will do leave the Creature burning for 30 seconds dealing 1d6 fire damage. 

the tar lasts until the creature dies or if you cast the spell again.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The amount of sentient tar you can have goes up by 1 when you reach levels 5 (2), 11 (3), and 17 (4).
