# Wanderling Vagrents
Huge ooze,
- **Armor Class:** 16 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 541 (31d12) +340
- **Speed:** fly 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA    |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------ |
| 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 20 (+5) | 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 6 (-2) |
- **Saving Throws:** Dex +8, Con +10
- **Damage Resistances:** acid, poison
- **Condition Immunities:** blinded, charmed
- **Senses:** passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 13 (10,000 XP)
## Ability's
#### Overcharge.
 When low HP(150hp) it will start overcharging for 2 turns, will hit every one within a 360ft range if they or not in cover from the blast dealing 4d10+6 lighting damage
#### Neuro Plasma.
 Whenever the Wanderling Vagrents starts its turn with half hit points or fewer gain advantage on all attack rolls
 
---
## Actions
#### Nematocysts.
 Shoots out 6 nematocysts balls. Ranged Attack: +4 to hit, reach 60ft,multy target. Hit:1d8+4 poison damage
 
#### Tentacle sweep.
 Makes twee Melee Attack: +4 to hit, reach 20ft. one target. Hit: 2d6+4 Budging damage.
 ---
## Bonus Actions
#### Charge.
 the Wanderling Vagrents can move up to its speed toward a hostile creature that it can see.
#### Corrosive Membrane.
 If a creature starts its turn within 10 ft. of the Wanderling Vagrents the Wanderling Vagrents can force the creature to make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw if the Wanderling Vagrents isn't incapacitated. On a failed save, the creature takes 3d4 Acid Damage or half that on a success. in addition if the creature failed its saving throw its speed is reduced by 20ft
