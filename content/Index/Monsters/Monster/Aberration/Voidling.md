# Voidling (Fase 1)
Gargantuan aberration (Void),
- **Armor Class:** 19 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 258 (12d20 + 132)
- **Speed:** 60 ft.

| STR      | DEX     | CON      | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
| -------- | ------- | -------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 36 (+13) | 29 (+9) | 35 (+12) | 27 (+7) | 18 (+4) | 11 (+0) |
- **Saving Throws:** Dex +14, Con +17, Int +14
- **Damage Immunities:** necrotic, Void
- **Condition Immunities:** paralyzed, petrified, prone, unconscious
- **Senses:** blindsight 30 ft., passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** telepathy 60 ft.
- **Challenge:** 20 (25,000 XP)
## Ability's
#### Multiattack.
 The Voidling makes two attacks.
#### Void Absorption.
 Whenever the Voidling is subjected to Necrotic damage, it takes no damage and instead regains a number of hit points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.
#### Interceptor.
 The Voidling has two initiatives
 
---
### Actions
#### tentacle Strike.
 _tentacle Strike:_ +15 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 21 (3d8 + 9) Void damage.
#### Void Artillery.
 The Voidling Shoots out 2d6 Void Artillery shots to any place within 90ft of him, every creature targeted by this attack must make a DC 20 Dex saving throw or 1d8 void damage per shot on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. in addition if a creature fails that creature takes 1d4 void damage at the end of their turn, until they gain hp or temp hp.
 
after used (and after every turn if Voidling does not have this action) roll a d8 if 6,7 or 8 Recharge this action

### Bonus Actions
#### Void Fog
50ft away till 500ft there is void fog, every creature in this fox must make a DC 22 Con saving throw or take 4d8 Void Damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. in addition if a creature fails that creature loses 2 AC until they long rest
the fog lasts for 1hour or until Voiding dies.
