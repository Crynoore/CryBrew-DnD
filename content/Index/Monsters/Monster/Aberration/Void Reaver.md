# Void Reaver
Large aberration (Void),
- **Armor Class:** 19 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 198 (12d10 + 132)
- **Speed:** 60 ft.

| STR      | DEX     | CON      | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
| -------- | ------- | -------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 30 (+10) | 26 (+8) | 32 (+11) | 18 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 11 (+0) |
- **Saving Throws:** Dex +14, Con +17, Int +10
- **Damage Immunities:** necrotic
- **Condition Immunities:** paralyzed, petrified, prone, unconscious
- **Senses:** blindsight 30 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** telepathy 60 ft.
- **Challenge:** 20 (25,000 XP)
## Ability's
#### Multiattack.
 The Void Reaver makes two attacks.
#### Implosion.
 When the Void Reaver dies, a delayed implosion will instantly kill anything within a 15ft radius 6 seconds later.(they are Transported to the Void but are presumed to be dead)
#### Void Absorption.
 Whenever the Void Reaver is subjected to Necrotic damage, it takes no damage and instead regains a number of hit points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.
 
---
### Actions
#### tentacle Strike.
 _tentacle Strike:_ +14 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 21 (3d8 + 8) Void damage.
#### Void Artillery.
 The Void Reaver Shoots out 2d6 Void Artillery shots to any place within 90ft of him, every creature targeted by this attack must make a DC 20 Dex saving throw or 1d8 void damage per shot on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. in addition if a creature fails that creature takes 1d4 void damage at the end of their turn, until they gain hp or temp hp.

after used (and after every turn if Void Reaver does not have this action) roll a d8 if 6,7 or 8 Recharge this action

---
### Bonus Actions
#### Blink.

 The Void Reaver magically teleports, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying, up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.
