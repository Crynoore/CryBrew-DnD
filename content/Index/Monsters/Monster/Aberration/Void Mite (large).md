# Void Mite
Large aberration (Void),
- **Armor Class:** 19 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 92 (8d10 + 48)
- **Speed:** 50 ft., climb 50 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 16 (+3) | 26 (+8) | 23 (+6) | 28 (+9) | 18 (+4) | 13 (+1) |
- **Saving Throws:** Dex +14, Con +12, Int +15
- **Damage Immunities:** necrotic
- **Condition Immunities:** paralyzed, petrified, prone, unconscious
- **Senses:** blindsight 30 ft., passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** 
- **Challenge:** 17 (18,000 XP)
## Ability's
#### Multiattack.
 The Void Mite makes tree attacks.
#### Implosion.
 When the Void Mite dies, a delayed implosion will instantly kill anything within a 10ft radius 6 seconds later.(they are Transported to the Void but are presumed to be dead)
#### Void Absorption.
 Whenever the Void Mite is subjected to Necrotic damage, it takes no damage and instead regains a number of hit points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.
 ---
### Actions
#### tentacle Strike.
 _tentacle Strike:_ +14 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 21 (3d8 + 8) Void damage.
 ---
### Bonus Actions
#### Blink.
 The Void Mite magically teleports, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying, up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.
