# Void Mite
Small aberration (Void),
- **Armor Class:** 15 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 39 (6d6 + 18)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA    |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------ |
| 12 (+1) | 18 (+4) | 17 (+3) | 19 (+4) | 10 (+0) | 8 (-1) |
- **Saving Throws:** Dex +7, Con +6, Int +7
- **Damage Immunities:** necrotic
- **Condition Immunities:**paralyzed, petrified, prone, unconscious
- **Senses:** blindsight 30 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** 
- **Challenge:** 7 (2,900 XP)
## Ability's
#### Multiattack.
 The Void Mite makes two attacks.
#### Implosion.
 When the Void Mite dies, a delayed implosion will instantly kill anything within a 5ft radius 6 seconds later.(they are Transported to the Void but are presumed to be dead)
#### Void Absorption.
 Whenever the Void Mite is subjected to Necrotic damage, it takes no damage and instead regains a number of hit points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.
 ---
### Actions
#### tentacle Strike.
 _tentacle Strike:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 17 (3d8 + 4) Void damage.
 ---
### Bonus Actions
#### Blink.
 The Void Mite magically teleports, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying, up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.
