# Catachan Brute
Huge beast,
- **Armor Class:** 19 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 275 (22d12 + 132)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., burrow 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX         | CON     | INT    | WIS     | CHA    |
| ------- | ----------- | ------- | ------ | ------- | ------ |
| 17 (+3) | 16 (+3)<br> | 22 (+6) | 9 (-1) | 12 (+2) | 6 (-2) |
- **Saving Throws:** Str +7, Con +10
- **Damage Resistances:** acid, poison
- **Condition Immunities:** charmed, poisoned
- **Senses:** darkvision 50 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** -
- **Challenge:** 9 (5,000 XP)

## Ability's
#### Death Pheromones.
 when the Catachan Brute dies any other Monster in the Catachan Brute family gets a +2 to its AC and a +1 to all of its attack rolls
#### Grappler.
 The Catachan Brute has advantage on attack rolls against any creature grappled by it.
#### Repercussions.
 The Catachan Brute has advantage on attack rolls against any creature That got effected by the Aura of Fear

---
## Actions
#### Stinger Strike.
 Stinger Strike: _+7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target._ Hit: _3d6+7 piercing damage + 2d8 poison damage._
#### Catachan Smash.
 Catachan Smash: _+5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target._ Hit: _3d8 bludgeoning damage and -2 AC to the Creatures of that got attack till the end of Catachan Brute next turn._

---
## Bonus Actions
#### Distil Fear.
 Aura of Fear: all enemy's in a 60ft radius must make a Str saving throw of 16 if they fail they are in fear and have disadvantage on there attacks for the next 30 seconds.

recharges: Roll a 6d at the end of its turn and on a 5,6 it recharges

---
## Layer Action
#### Fear Pheromone 
at the end of initiative a cloud of Pheromone pushes closer to the Catachan Brute starting 160ft away and coming closer by 30ft. if a creature is in this cloud it takes 2d6 Force damage and is in fear till they attack.
