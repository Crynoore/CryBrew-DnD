# Catachan Devil
Large beast,
- **Armor Class:** 15 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 142 (15d10 + 60)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., burrow 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX         | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA    |
| ------- | ----------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------ |
| 16 (+3) | 16 (+3)<br> | 18 (+4) | 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 6 (-2) |
- **Saving Throws:** Str +6, Con +7
- **Damage Resistances:** acid, poison
- **Condition Immunities:** charmed, poisoned
- **Senses:** darkvision 50 ft., passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP)

## Ability's
#### Death Pheromones.
 when the Catachan Devil dies any other Monster in the Catachan Devil family gets a +2 to its AC and a +1 to all of its attack rolls.
 
 ---
## Actions
#### Stinger Strike.
 Stinger Strike: _+6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target._ Hit: _2d6+3 piercing damage + 1d8 poison damage._
#### Catachan Smash.
 Catachan Smash: _+4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target._ Hit: _2d8 bludgeoning damage and -1 AC to the Creatures of that got attack till the end of Catachan Devil next turn._
 ---
## Bonus Actions
#### Aggressive.
 As a bonus action, the Catachan Devil can move up to its speed toward a hostile creature that it can see.
#### Pheromones of Fear.
 when Catachan Devil reaches half hp it can releases a Pheromones, all enemy's in a 60ft radius must make a Str saving throw of 16 if they fail they are in fear and have disadvantage on there next attack

recharges: Roll a 6d at the end of its turn and on a 5,6 it recharges
