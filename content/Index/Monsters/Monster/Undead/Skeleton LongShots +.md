# Skeleton Bonemeranger
Medium undead,
- **Armor Class:** 18 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 77 (14d8 + 14)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA    |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------ |
| 16 (+3) | 22 (+6) | 13 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 8 (-1) |
- **Saving Throws:** Dex +11
- **Damage Resistances:** poison
- **Condition Immunities:** exhaustion, poisoned
- **Senses:** passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** understands Dwarvish but cant speak
- **Challenge:** 13 (10,000 XP)
## Ability's
#### Dead eye.
Skeleton Bonemeranger ranged attacks with weapons ignore Half Cover and Three-Quarters Cover.

#### Multiattack.
 The Skeleton Bonemeranger makes two attacks.
