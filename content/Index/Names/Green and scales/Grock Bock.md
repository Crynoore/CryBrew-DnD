a character for [[green-and-scales]]


Borne in the solid corridors of Stonevein Hold, Grock Bock was a determined miner but not the smartest, always the first to tunnel deeper into the earth. One day, in his pursuit of a valuable vein of ore, he dug too deep. and the enters collapsed, crushing him in darkness. Thirty years of hard, Training sustained by sheer determination, he honed his body and will against stone and silence. Every punch of his fists, every roar against the overwhelming darkness became his habit.

When Grock finally broke free, his triumph was reduced to dust. Stonevein Hold was lost—its corridors in ruins, its inhabitants slaughtered. The culprits were no meier beast, but men: the Guild of the Eastern Flame, who razed his homeland.

Now, Grock roams the surface bruised but unbroken, his rage stoked by tragedy. He takes vengeance on the Guild, one butchered sword and shattered skull at a time.
