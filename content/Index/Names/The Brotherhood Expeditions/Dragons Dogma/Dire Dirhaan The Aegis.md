Character from [[The Brotherhood Expeditions]] played by [[Deanamo]].
Pronounced "Dirhaan The Aegis"

This is the tale of Dire and Jun, a tale of two human boys who dreamed of making it big in the world. Yet just like every story, it ends in tragedy.
  
Dire and Jun were the bestest of friends, almost like brothers, boys born into the second generation after The Great Evil from which their grandparents fled. Their youth was tough with everyone still settling in and the rising tension. Along this their grandmother was slowly going crazy, her mind twisted and traumatised by the great evil. Luckily through it all they had each other and a noble group of heroes to protect them. “The Imperial Brotherhood” they were called, Dire and Jun looked up to these heroes and dreamed of nothing more than to one day join them and so they did. The day they turned 18 they signed up and officially entered The Brotherhood as initiates. They learned quickly and the duo gained quite the name. Until one dreadful day, the sixth day of an operation called “The Reconnaissance of Calamity Canyon”. Times were tough and the duo was looking rough, Jun the Valiant and Dire the Enchanted were deep in the canyon. Their squad got overwhelmed by a cave in and in the heat of it all falling stalactite ended Jun’s time at The Brotherhood. If it wasn’t for the squad pulling Dire along he would have suffered a similar fate, he was distraught. A friend, a brother, someone he grew up with had met his end. Then things changed…
 
For one story had ended in tragedy, a new one was about to begin.

Dire devoted his life to Abjuration, a school of magic solely focussed on the creation of barriers and wards. He swore that as long as he lives no life of meaning would be lost.
 
Some Factoids:

- Although covered by his beard he carries Jun’s IB Insignia in a locker around his neck
- After the incident he became somewhat of a recluse. Afraid of the past repeating he tries to avoid getting too personal with his colleagues. This does however not influence his promise to protect.
- Through his studies he learned to colour his magic an Eminence purple to honour Jun’s favourite childhood stuffed toy: Byzant.
- Dire took on the title of “The Aegis” to reflect his new goal of protection instead of his generic cool-at-the-time sounding title of “the Enchanted”
- He might seem gruff and unfriendly but deep down does care and will ALWAYS have your back, front and everything inbetween.
- Dire is currently 26 years old and has been sent out on a new mission together with a Gnome Fighter, Dragonborn Paladin and Orc Cleric.
