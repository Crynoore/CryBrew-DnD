Character from [[The Brotherhood Expeditions]] 

Parmigiano Reggiano, once he was a free dragonborn without a worry in the world. He had loving parents and sold cheese for a living. The business went well, since he had a way to persuade the people that his cheese was a must in their home. Outside of business hours, he wanted to train like a soldier. His father saved up and got him his very own flail and fighting lessons when he became an adult. As he grew up, he pledged his service to Helm, the vigilant one, promising to punish the unjust and helping those in need. Items that didn’t sell in store were given to the poor who needed food, and 3 sun rotations ago, Parmigiano offered his services to the brotherhood to let justice rule and to keep the peace if possible.
