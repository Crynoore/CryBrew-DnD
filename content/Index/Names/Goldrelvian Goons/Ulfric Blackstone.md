## Backstory

A note from Ulfric's diary: 

As a small dwarf of about 30 years old, the hill where I lived with my brothers and sisters was taken over by the brass Dragonborn.

We lived and worked there as slaves for years. After a risky plan, my brother Yngwie and I escaped and took an oath to take revenge on the Dragonborn and free our brothers and sisters from tyranny. This happened when we were 100 years old.
Since then, we have both been working on improving our skills, but here we have taken a different path. I ended up at a church and have now been trained as a paladin for 20 years.
I am not 100% sure of what Yngwie is doing, but I do know that it is not according to the will of God.

We have agreed to see each other again in the near future; I hope everything goes well and look forward to our reunion.
