Character from [[The Recrudesence of Goldrelf]] played by Mico

## Backstory

Yngwie, born from the same rock as his brother [[Ulfric Blackstone]] was subjected to the same fate of ensalvement. However their plan for escape was not flawless, it meant he had to leave his wife and four kids alone. Ygnwie did what he had to do and left them behind without looking back. After escaping he planned with his brother to meet up after some time and to one day take revenge on those who oppressed their folk.

Some time passed and Yngwie had found himself a new wife (although late genderswapped by a wizard) and a passion for thievery. Yet deep inside he still cared for his people and decided to do some spywork. They were spotted and got chased, during this chase Yngwie tripped over a green dragonborn and from that day on his life changed it's course forever.
