Character from [[The Recrudesence of Goldrelf]] played by [[Hypno]].

## Backstory

In the Hermit Woods lays a green egg glistening in the sun. One of the treasures of That, Braurza and Ikra procured from their recent heist of a dragon’s lair to the south of the woods. The egg slowly began to shake, hatching a little green dragon. The lizardfolk took the little dragon in as one of their own, calling him "the dragon" of the pack. After hearing the dragon so many times, the child chose his name. From then on he would be called Dergn, because it sounds like “dragon”. Dumbwitted as they might be the Scaled Reapers realised they could not control this dragon once it would grow up. Luckily for them a shady merchant has acquire the perfect tool for the job, the Tag of Concealment.

Growing up Dergn saw his lizard friends as his true family, never knowing his true heritage, even being happy he had been stolen as an egg. On his 2th birthday, Dergn received necklace with a circular blue fluorescent crystal from his peers, a symbol of the group. 2 sickles were also given to him as a weapon. Carved out of the first treasure they stole together with Dergn, being a big blue crystal. As hunters and gatherers the Lizardtribe went around collecting as much treasure and food as they could. Slowly building a name for themselves because they'd seek out weakened adventurers to kill and eat as a source of nutrients. The lizardfolk were pretty apathetic, only caring for themselves. Dergn adopted this trait. After travelling for multiple years together they reached the woods of neverwinter when a fateful day struck. All of the killing of adventures had put a bounty on their heads. An Elven huntress who lived in the neverwinter capital knew of their bounty had spotted them in the woods. The elven hunter followed them deep within the woods where she cornered them whilst still being hidden in the canopies of the tree. Suddenly an arrow pierced Ikra's body. Before the troop could react, the elven huntress appeared in front of them, drawing an elvish shortsword. Braurza reached for his sickle while he screamed "Run!" to That and Dergn

While running Dergn didn't look at where his feet were landing, resulting in him stumbling into a ravine. The loose rocks made Dergn fall, cutting his left hand and arm in the process. When Dergn stood back on his two feet he saw a shiny golden scroll, leaking a purple fluid, that when touching the ground created a veiny structure out which mushroom like bodies grew. Out of fascination, Dergn touched one of the mushrooms, forgetting that his primary hand was cut. By touching the mushroom a shock of pain went through his body. Hallucinations began to surround Dergn, while his arm and eye sockets turned a glowing purple hue. "Grab the scroll... Grab the SCROLLLL", with these words echoing through his head, Dergn reached for the scroll. Text began to appear on the once blank slate in a language Dergn could understand (Lizardspeak). Without hesitation Dergn rolled up the scroll and stashed it on his belt, next to the two sickles he got from his kin. Simultaneously,l a gigantic shadowy figure appeared in front of Dergn (The hexdragon Goddess [[Goldrelf]]), filling him with dread. But before Dergn could even process the sight, the shape disappeared. After taking the scroll of its rocky pedestal, the veins together with the mushrooms started to retract while the purple fluid dried up. Dergn quickly gathered his four glass vails and filled them up with the fading fluid. Feeling overwhelmed by what just happened, Dergn looked for a way out of the cavern. After a few hours of walking, Dergn found his way out. Whilst the daylight carressed Dergn's scales, he collapsed on the soft grass of the Woods of Neverwinter. Not much later he would meet a Dwarf that would change the course of his life
