Character from [[The Recrudesence of Goldrelf]] played by Ayden

## Backstory

Nikola Alaric, the youngest scion of the Alaric family, was expected to carry on this prestigious tradition. From a young age, he was immersed in the world of crafting, surrounded by the hum of enchanted forges and the glow of magical runes. However, unlike his older brothers, Victor and Magnus, Nikola struggled to master the intricate techniques of crafting. His hands, skilled in wielding weapons, faltered when it came to the delicate work demanded by the family's trade.
  
Despite Nikola's shortcomings, his grandfather Eldrion saw potential in his unique talents. Eldrion recognized the value of martial skill in a world filled with danger and turmoil. He often spoke up in Nikola's defense, advocating for a more inclusive view of the family's legacy and teaching him skills like the language of the dwarves.

However, Viktor Alaric, driven by the rigid traditions of the family, could not look past Nikola's perceived inadequacies. In a fateful confrontation, against the protests of Eldrion, Viktor made the difficult decision to expel Nikola from the Alaric family, asserting that he was unfit to carry the family name and reputation.
 
Heartbroken and rejected by his own father, Nikola left Home with a heavy heart. He wandered about until he joined the Harpers, using his talent with weapons to help out where he could. It was when [[Neverwinter]] was attacked by a large frog that he met a certain dwarven paladin and the coarse of his life changes forever.
