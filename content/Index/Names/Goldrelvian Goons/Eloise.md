Retired character from [[The Recrudesence of Goldrelf]] played by Julie

## Backstory

Eloise was born in 1593 as the youngest of 5 children in Waterdeep. Her Mother, Nia, and her father, Yord, were her number one fans from the start. Her other siblings were also very nice to her. She had 2 older brothers and 2 older sisters. The oldest one was her wise brother Edward. He is very smart and is now a powerful sorcerer. He always protected tiny Eloise from people who meant harm to her. Even the people who just were joking around. Edward was sometimes a bit overprotective. Then came Eva, she was very active. Always running around and learning new tricks like backflips. Eva and Eloise were never really close due to Eva not being home most of the times. Eva wanted to see the world. Eleonora was just like Edward very smart and productive. She was way less charming or charismatic thought… Eleonora is the reader of the family and even wrote a few stories of her own. Ethan and Eloise were best friends. Ethan is only one year older than Eloise which made them perfect playbuddies. They did a lot of wrong but their loving parents never were really mad at them.
