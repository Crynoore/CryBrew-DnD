Character from [[The Recrudesence of Goldrelf]] played by Anaïs

## Backstory

I am Lia Meliamne. I grew up in a small forest village in Neverwinter. We protect our territory from strangers and we live in little houses in the trees, high and low. My house is quite high up; here I can sit with the birds and listen to their singing all day long. I have had some animal friends from a young age because I quickly learned to communicate with animals and feel at one with the forest. I have a cat named Lariana and a sparrow named Mira. My favorite things are exploring and relaxing in my little hut. My parents don't allow me to leave the forest, so unfortunately I have already discovered everything; nowadays I often sit surrounded by the animals in the trees. I would prefer to stay here in my little hut and enjoy the sounds of nature, but I often have to help gather berries, nuts, and mushrooms. As you can hear, my life is very quiet, and also quite boring. Until, while gathering food, I heard the noise of a gang that made my life a lot more interesting and hectic...
