character for [[The Long Way Home]]

shares a backstory with [[Suute'ra]]

Prolog: Glynhorn Alito di gelo made love with a daughter of the Perhe family(gnome’s), while his was dating Kvinnlig Avkomma for 4 years he got the information of a lost Strider Airship, after hearing of this he dipped and went to look for the ship leaving the Perhe family behind.

  

Glynhorn Alito di gelo was looking for the ship for 12 month passing the city of jääkauden kaupunki as on of his last stops 

After one of his longer expeditions he finally stumbled upon the frozen Strider Airship.

  

This Airship was to his surprise not vacant, there was a small family of Rimekin there (or locally known as kuurin sukua), he was careful not to provoke this elegant creatures and after 3 weeks he got the main part of the ship back into usable stade. And started to share it with the rimekin. Although communicating with these creatures was hard, they often annoyed him by just staring at him while he was working on the ship. After a good while the ship was up and running and Glynhorn even got on the Rimekin to help him maintain the ship.

  

While Glynhorn was roaming around, flying through the air having the time of his life, except when he needed to work on the ship. Lucky for him one of the Rimekin was quite handy at maintaining the ship and quick surpassed Glynhorn and white this they stared to roam the air ways for a long time Glynhorn teaching the Rimekin Elvish and giving him the name [[Suute'ra]]
