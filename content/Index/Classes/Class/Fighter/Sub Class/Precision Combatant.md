## Level 3: Precision Strike


when you attack you can roll a extra d12 (Precision)
you have double your proficiency bonus as Precision Dice
(you need to roll this at the same time a you roll to hit)

| Dice number | Part      | Effect                                                             |
| ----------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 12          | Neck      | your attack deals 2d12 extra dmg (you need to hit)                 |
| 11          | Head      | the Crit threshold for this creature is lower by 1 for 1 min       |
| 10          | Chest     | Creature takes 2d6 dmg                                             |
| 9           | Chest     | Creature takes 2d6 dmg                                             |
| 8           | Shoulders | Creature gets disadvantage on his attack for 1 min                 |
| 7           | Abdomen   | Creature gets disadvantage on his saving throws for the next 1 min |
| 6           | Abdomen   | Creature gets disadvantage on his saving throws for the next 1 min |
| 5           | Arms      | Creature drops his weapon                                          |
| 4           | Arms      | Creature drops his weapon                                          |
| 3           | Groin     | Creature takes 2d4 dmg                                             |
| 2           | Legs      | Creature has half movement for 10 min                              |
| 1           | Foot      | Creature fall prone                                                |

## Level 7: Elite Knowledge
- you gain proficiency in one skill of your choice from the skills available to Fighters at level 1.
- in conversation you find the right word that people want to hear.
if you are doing a charisma check you can roll it with advantage = to you proficiency bonus + you Strength or Dexterity Modifier (with one is high)
