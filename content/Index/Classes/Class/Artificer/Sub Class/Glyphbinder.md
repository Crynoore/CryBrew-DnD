### Level 3: 
When you reach an Artificer level specified in the Glyphbinder cantrip table, you gain access to these cantrips

| Artificer Level | Cantrips                                                             |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 3               | choose a cantrip form the Artificer list                             |
| 5               | choose a cantrip form the Artificer or wizard list                   |
| 9               | choose a cantrip form the Artificer, wizard or Sorcerer list         |
| 13              | choose a cantrip form the Artificer, wizard, Sorcerer or Cleric list |
| 17              | choose a cantrip form the All lists                                  |
