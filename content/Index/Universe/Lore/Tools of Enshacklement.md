There are currently 3 known cases of Enshacklement magic being used to craft objects. These items are based on design made by “[[The Dawnsmith]]”
- The five rings used to seal Goldrelf made by [[The Dawnsmith]] together with Ezahl and Agatha
- The Tag of Concealment made by The Dawnsmith’s son and stolen by bandits. 
- The Insignia of Enshacklement, the “perfect” version of the design smithed by [[Galiminus]] and [[Cyrus]] with [[Cosmic Brass]] supplied from [[Yagor]]

A grand darkness was sweeping over the sword coast, Ezahl and Agatha were in desperate need of a way to seal Goldrelf. Together with the help of [[The Dawnsmith]] they designed and crafted five rings to fulfill this purpose. Not long after finishing this magnum opus [[The Dawnsmith]] passed leaving his son behind who dedicated his life to continue his father’s legacy. He worked tirelessly to create a prototype only to get overwhelmed by bandits trying to steal such a desirable item. He was able to survive the encounter but the bandits got away with his “Tag of Concealment”. This item eventually ended up in the hands of a gang by the name of “The Scaled Reapers”, the were a troublesome bunch who had hatched a green dragon wyrmling from a stolen egg. Using this Tag they were able to shift this wyrmling into a being resembling a dragonbron. This dragonbron would later be known as Dergn the Dragon, a name he would only later discover held his true self
