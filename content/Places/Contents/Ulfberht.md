Long ago, before the rise of Waterdeep, before the cataclysms of Goldrelf, the continent of the sword coast was many times vaster than it deems today. This landmass was known as Faerun but was in fact not a single titan, in truth it were twins entangled in eachothers being. They drifted the world together before some unknown event occurred forcing them apart. Many theorise what this event could have been but no proof has been found as of yet. This mystery of history has been dubbed “The Unsheathing” as one of the twins, the one I currently reside on is named Ulfberht after its resemblance of a sword. With the “Sword Coast” its blade and “The Spine of the World” its hilt this titan has been alive for years upon years, but what came of the “sheathe” where has the other part of these Titan twins gone? 
~A pondering soul
