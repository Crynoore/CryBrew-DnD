Shadow of the Eclipse
	level 3 spell 
	Casting Time: 1 action
	**Components:** V, S
	duration : 1min
	Range: 60 ft

You curse a creature with the eclipse’s shadow.
Target makes a WIS save. On fail:
At the start of each of its turns, roll a d6:
1–3 (Solar Burn): it takes 2d6 radiant damage.
4–6 (Lunar Wither): it takes 2d6 necrotic damage.
While cursed, the creature cant see further then 20ft
