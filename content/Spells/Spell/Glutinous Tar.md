| Glutinous Tar                    |
| -------------------------------- |
| **Casting Time:** 1 bonus action |
| **Range:** 120 feet              |
| **Components:** V, S             |
| **Spell level:**0                |

summens a pool of tar under a enemy the radius of the pool is 20ft
if enemy is in this tar they are in difficult terrain
(the tar is indeed very flammable)
