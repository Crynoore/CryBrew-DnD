- 7th level Evocation
- Casting Time: 1 Action
- Range: Self (100 by 20-foot line)
- Components: V
- Duration: Instantaneous
- Classes: Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock

You emit an invisible force forming a line 100 feet long and 20 feet wide. Each creature in the area must make a strength saving throw. On a success, nothing happens. On a failure, the creature takes 8d6 force damage and is pushed back to, from where you are standing, the opposite end of the spell’s 100 foot line. If a creature is pushed back into a sturdy object, like a boulder or a stone wall, and they have not been pushed back a total of 100 feet yet, they take an additional 1d6 force damage for every remaining 10 feet.

If a creature is brought to 0 hp by this spell they are killed. Their body is liquified in an unrecognizable mass from the extreme and sudden pressure.
