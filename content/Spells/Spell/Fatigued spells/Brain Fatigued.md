| Brain Fatigued                 |
| ------------------------------ |
| Casting Time: 1 action         |
| Range: 30 ft                   |
| Components: V, S               |
| Spell level: ?                 |
| Accelerated cut-off point : 28 |
| Accelerated Dice : D12         |

Targets a creature within 30ft and then can target another creature with 30ft of the first’s target’s location and then can target another creature with 30ft of the first’s target’s location 

This effect has a cut-off point of ”28”. When activated, the creature must roll dice until their total equals or exceeds 28. Each roll is: Die roll + INT modifier .You must keep rolling until the cut-off is reached, no matter how many dice it takes.

NoD = to the amount of turns you can’t concentrate on something
