## Hollowed frame

This effect has a cut-off point of ”30”. When activated, the creature must roll dice until their total equals or exceeds 30. Each roll is: Die roll + CON modifier .You must keep rolling until the cut-off is reached, no matter how many dice it takes.

the amount of rolls is = to the amount of AC you lose for 30 sec
