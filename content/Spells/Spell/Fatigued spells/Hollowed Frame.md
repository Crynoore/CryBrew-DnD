Hollowed frame
	Casting Time: 1 action
	Range: 30 ft 
	Components: V, S 
	Spell level: ?
	Accelerated cut-off point : 11
	Accelerated Dice : D6

This effect has a cut-off point of ”11”. When activated, the creature must roll dice until their total equals or exceeds 11. Each roll is: Die roll + CON modifier .You must keep rolling until the cut-off is reached, no matter how many dice it takes.

NoD = to the amount of AC you lose for 30 sec
