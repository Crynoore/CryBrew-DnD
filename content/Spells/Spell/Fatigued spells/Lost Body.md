Lost Body
	Casting Time: 1 action
	Range: 30 ft 
	Components: V, S 
	Spell level: ?
	Accelerated cut-off point : 28
	Accelerated Dice : D12

This effect has a cut-off point of ”28”. When activated, the creature must roll dice until their total equals or exceeds 28. Each roll is: Die roll + WIS modifier .You must keep rolling until the cut-off is reached, no matter how many dice it takes.

| NoD       | effect for 30 seconds ( 5 turns)                        |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 or less | deal 1d4 less dmg                                       |
| 3         | gain disadvantage on attack rolls                       |
| 4 or more | gain disadvantage on attack rolls and deal 1d4 less dmg |
