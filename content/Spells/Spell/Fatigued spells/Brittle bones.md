## Brittle bones

This effect has a cut-off point of ”30”. When activated, the creature must roll dice until their total equals or exceeds 30. Each roll is: Die roll + STR modifier .You must keep rolling until the cut-off is reached, no matter how many dice it takes.


the amount of rolls is = to the amount of dmg you lose on attacks for 30 sec
