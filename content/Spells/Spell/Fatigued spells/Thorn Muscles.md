| Thorn Muscles                  |
| ------------------------------ |
| Casting Time: 1 action         |
| Range: 30 ft                   |
| Components: V, S               |
| Spell level: ?                 |
| Accelerated cut-off point : 26 |
| Accelerated Dice : D8          |

This effect has a cut-off point of ”26”. When activated, the creature must roll dice until their total equals or exceeds 26. Each roll is: Die roll + DEX modifier .You must keep rolling until the cut-off is reached, no matter how many dice it takes.

NoD = to the amount of turns your movement speed is reduced to 10 ft
