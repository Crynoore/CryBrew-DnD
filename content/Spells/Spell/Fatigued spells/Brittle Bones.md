| Brittle bones                  |
| ------------------------------ |
| Casting Time: 1 action         |
| Range: 30 ft                   |
| Components: V, S               |
| Spell level: 2                 |
| Accelerated cut-off point : 34 |
| Accelerated Dice : D12         |

This effect has a cut-off point of ”34”. When activated, the creature must roll dice until their total equals or exceeds 34. Each roll is: Die roll + STR modifier .You must keep rolling until the cut-off is reached, no matter how many dice it takes.

NoD = to the amount of dmg you lose on attacks for 30 sec
